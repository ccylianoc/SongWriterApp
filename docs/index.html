Guide

✍️ Lyrics

Just type. Line numbers are automatic — backspace at the start of a line keeps it, empty lines delete normally.

🎸 Chords

Put your cursor on a word, click Chord, type e.g. Am — it floats above that word. Use − / + to transpose all chords.

📄 Sections

Click Section and type a name like Verse or Chorus to add a label.

🎼 Tabs

Guitar Tab / Bass Tab inserts tablature at your cursor. To remove one, backspace at the start of the line below it.

🎙️ Recordings

Hit REC to capture a melody, or Upload an audio file. Playback lives right here.

💾 Projects

Everything auto-saves to your library. Rename by clicking the title up top, delete with the trash icon. Save also downloads an HTML copy.

⚙️ Settings

Theme, accent color, font size, font family, and text color — all in the gear menu.
